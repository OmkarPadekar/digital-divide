A better question

Is a connection enough?

Traditional access statistics can hide the people who are still left behind. A more useful view follows the journey from infrastructure to real-life outcomes.

01

Infrastructure

Reliable networks, electricity and usable devices make digital participation possible.

02

Digital literacy

Skills and confidence help people search, communicate, create and stay safe online.

03

Affordability

Sustainable costs keep a connection active instead of forcing households to ration it.

04

Outcomes

The real test is whether access improves learning, work, services and everyday choice.
