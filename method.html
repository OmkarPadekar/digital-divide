The framework

Four questions make a fuller picture.

The framework follows the path from a physical connection to the benefits people can actually feel in their lives.

01

Infrastructure

Is a stable, fast enough connection available, alongside a device that can use it?

Access
02

Digital literacy

Can people search, communicate, create, protect themselves and solve problems online?

Capability
03

Affordability

Can a household keep the connection active without trading away essentials?

Sustainability
04

Socio-economic outcomes

Does digital access improve education, work, health, services or participation?

Impact
The calculation

Layered score = the average of all four layers.

Infrastructure+Literacy+Affordability+Outcomes÷4

Values in the explorer are illustrative estimates designed to make the relationship between layers easier to understand. Replace them with sourced indicators when using this framework for research.
